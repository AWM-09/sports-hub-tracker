# AlexMyrusTalksSports Hub

A home for the sports trackers and projects made by AlexMyrusTalksSports.

## Trackers

- [Football & Soccer Stats Tracker](https://awm-09.github.io/football-stats-tracker/) — Fixtures, tables, teams, players, match stats, head-to-heads and records across selected leagues and competitions.
- [Formula Stats Tracker](https://awm-09.github.io/formula-series-stats-tracker/) — Formula 1 historical seasons, driver and constructor standings, race results and comparisons.
- [NFL Stats Tracker](https://awm-09.github.io/nfl-stats-tracker/) — NFL stats, schedules, player and team pages, analysis tools and more.
- [NHL Stats Tracker](https://awm-09.github.io/nhl-stats-tracker/) — NHL stats, player profiles, team hubs, schedules, records and research tools.
- Volleyball Stats Tracker — Coming soon. [View the project on GitHub](https://github.com/AWM-09/volleyball-stats-tracker).
